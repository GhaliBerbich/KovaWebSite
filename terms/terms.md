## **15. Fare Guidelines**

These Fare Guidelines are intended to help Users understand how pricing works on the KOVA Platform in connection with Prearranged Rides and to allow Users to make informed decisions before requesting or providing transportation services.

1. **Fares.**
    1. **General Fare Structure.**
        
        Users acknowledge that the KOVA App may calculate and display fares for Prearranged Rides. KOVA does **not** charge Users for merely accessing or using the KOVA App or KOVA Network; charges apply **only** when a User books a Prearranged Ride through the Platform.
        
        Fares, when charged, are generally **non-refundable**, unless otherwise required under applicable law.
        
    2. **No Cash or External Payment Methods.**
        
        Users are strictly prohibited from requesting, accepting, or offering cash payments for any portion of a Prearranged Ride before, during, or after the trip.
        
        Users are also prohibited from using external card readers, mobile POS systems, or any third-party payment tools to collect payments for a ride.
        
        Any User who is asked to pay or accept such unauthorized payments should promptly notify KOVA.
        
    3. **Authorized Payment Channels Only.**
        
        All fare payments must be processed **solely through the KOVA App or KOVA Network**. KOVA may rely on approved third-party payment processors to handle fare collection, settlements, or transfers.
        
    4. **No Additional Charges for Disability-Related Needs.**
        
        In accordance with KOVA’s Accessibility and Nondiscrimination Policy, the Company will not permit any extra fees, surcharges, or penalties to be imposed on Users with disabilities for a Prearranged Ride.
        
    5. **Tips.**
        
        Users may choose to leave an optional gratuity to a Driver upon completion of a Prearranged Ride. Tips are voluntary, paid separately from the fare, and are fully passed through to the Driver.
        
    
    **2. Estimated and Actual Fare Calculations via the KOVA App**
    
    1. **Display of Estimated Fare.**
        
        Before confirming a Prearranged Ride, the KOVA App will show the User an estimated fare. The User may then decide whether to request the Ride after reviewing that estimate.
        
        This estimate reflects the pricing parameters active on the Platform at the time of the request.
        
        Because KOVA facilitates peer-to-peer transportation—and because ride costs may fluctuate with factors such as fuel prices, driver operating costs, and local market conditions—both estimated and actual fares are generated through an automated system available to both Riders and Drivers.
        
    2. **How Fares Are Calculated.**
        
        The KOVA App calculates both estimated and actual fares based on the pick-up point and intended destination submitted by the User.
        
        Estimated fares may vary depending on factors such as the time of day, demand, and regional fuel prices at the moment the ride is requested.
        
        Actual fares may differ based on the real-time duration and distance traveled during the Prearranged Ride.
        
    3. **Difference Between Estimated and Actual Fare.**
        
        The actual fare charged may be higher or lower than the initial estimate.
        
        Additionally, if the Rider modifies the drop-off location or route during the trip, the fare may adjust to reflect the updated ride details.
        
    
    **3. Electronic Receipts**
    
    1. **Receipt Delivery.**
        
        After each Prearranged Ride is completed, KOVA will issue an electronic receipt to all Users involved in the trip. Receipts will be accessible through the KOVA App and may also be delivered via other electronic formats.
        
    2. **Information Included in Receipts.**
        
        Each electronic receipt will generally include:
        
        1. an itemized breakdown of the fare associated with the Prearranged Ride (if applicable);
        2. the pick-up and destination locations; and
        3. the total time and distance traveled during the Prearranged Ride.
    
    **4. Promotions, Referrals, and Coupons**

    These promotional items:
    
    - have no cash value,

    - may only be redeemed within the KOVA App or KOVA Network.
    
    Unless explicitly stated otherwise in writing, promotional credits and coupons **may not be combined** for use on the same Prearranged Ride.
