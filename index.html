Common questions

Is KOVA only for Purdue students right now?
Yes, KOVA is at Purdue right now. More campuses are coming. If yours isn't listed yet, you can sign up to hear when it goes live.
How does pricing work if there's no commission?
Drivers set their own price per seat when they post a ride. Riders see the full cost upfront, no hidden fees. Whatever the driver charges, they keep.
Is it safe to ride with someone I don't know?
Every account is verified with a .edu email. Riders and drivers have profiles with photos and ratings. You can see who you're riding with before you confirm.
