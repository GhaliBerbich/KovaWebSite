Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 28, 2024

At Kova, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our rideshare platform.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

Name and surname

Email address (for students)

Phone number (for professional drivers)

University affiliation (for students)

Graduation year (for students)

Gender (optional)

Profile picture (optional)

1.2 Driver Verification Information

For drivers, we collect additional information for safety verification:

Driver's license information and images

Insurance documents

Employment verification (for professional drivers)

Driving history questionnaire responses

1.3 Referral Information

We generate a unique referral code for each user to track referrals within the platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

Create and manage your account

Verify driver credentials and ensure safety

Process referrals and track waitlist status

Communicate with you about your account

Improve our services and user experience

Comply with legal obligations

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Where We Store Your Data

All user data is stored securely using Google Firebase Cloud Firestore, which provides:

End-to-end encryption in transit (TLS/SSL)

Encryption at rest

Enterprise-grade security infrastructure

Regular security audits and compliance certifications

3.2 Authentication

We use Firebase Authentication for secure user login:

Email/password authentication with industry-standard encryption

Phone number verification using SMS codes

Email verification links sent securely

Passwords are never stored in plain text

3.3 Document Storage

Driver verification documents (license, insurance, employment proof) are stored as base64-encoded images in our secure Firestore database. These documents are:

Only accessible to authorized Kova administrators

Used solely for verification purposes

Encrypted at rest and in transit

Automatically deleted if your account is deleted

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4.1 Within the Platform

Limited information is shared with other users:

Your name and profile picture are visible to other users

Your university (for students)

Driver verification status (verified/not verified badge)

4.2 Service Providers

We work with trusted third-party service providers:

Firebase/Google Cloud: Data storage and authentication

Expo: Mobile app development and delivery

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, such as to:

Comply with legal processes (subpoenas, court orders)

Protect the rights and safety of Kova and its users

Prevent fraud or illegal activity

5. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

Access: View all personal information we have about you

Update: Edit your profile information at any time

Delete: Request deletion of your account and all associated data

Export: Request a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, contact us at help@ridekova.com or use the "Delete Account" option in your profile settings.

6. Account Deletion

When you delete your account:

Your profile is immediately removed from the platform

All personal information is permanently deleted from our databases

Driver verification documents are permanently deleted

Your referral code becomes inactive

This action cannot be undone

We may retain certain information for a limited period to comply with legal obligations or resolve disputes.

7. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we permanently delete your data within 30 days, except where we're required by law to retain it longer.

8. Children's Privacy

Kova is intended for users who are at least 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18. If we discover that we have collected information from a child under 18, we will delete it immediately.

9. International Users

Your information may be transferred to and stored on servers located in the United States. By using Kova, you consent to this transfer.

10. Email and Phone Verification

We use your email and phone number solely for:

Account verification

Password reset requests

Important account security notifications

Service updates (you can opt out)

We will never sell your contact information or send spam.

11. Cookies and Tracking

Our mobile app does not use cookies. We use Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with our app, which may collect:

Device type and operating system

App usage patterns (anonymized)

Crash reports to improve stability

This data is anonymized and cannot be used to identify you personally.

12. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by:

Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top

Sending you a notification within the app

Emailing you (for major changes)

13. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at:

Email: help@ridekova.com

14. Your Consent

By using Kova, you consent to this Privacy Policy and our collection, use, and storage of your information as described herein.

Kova is committed to protecting your privacy and maintaining the security of your personal information.
