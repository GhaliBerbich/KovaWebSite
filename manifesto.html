Get KOVA

You've seen it. The group chat fills up for a show, a game, a weekend trip — then one by one, people drop. No ride. Too expensive. No one to go with.

So you stay home and scroll instead.

Gen Z is stranded. The gap is almost always cost — and having no one to make the trip with.

KOVA exists because of that gap.

Rides between verified Purdue students, at prices you negotiate directly. No platform taking a cut. Just you and someone heading the same direction, sharing a trip, maybe more than that.

We built this so students show up to the concerts, the games, the festivals — and to each other.
