Q&A

How do I pay?
Drivers post a starting price, you text them directly to work out the details. Pay however works for you, Venmo, cash, whatever. KOVA doesn't handle payments or take a cut.
Is KOVA only for Purdue students right now?
Yes, KOVA is at Purdue right now. More campuses are coming. If yours isn't listed yet, you can sign up to hear when it goes live.
How does pricing work if there's no commission?
Drivers set their own price per seat. Riders see the full cost before confirming. The driver keeps all of it.
Is it safe to ride with someone I don't know?
Every account needs a .edu email to sign up. Riders and drivers both have profiles, photos, and ratings. You can see who you're riding with before you commit.
